## What changed

The Inbox now has one clear command-bar queue selector. Queue views and secondary filters are separated, the default Aguardando resposta view remains the V61 default, and only the trusted queue count is displayed. Conversation rows use a compact contact → metadata → preview hierarchy without a badge wall. Operational `Próxima` is distinct from `Próxima página`, while the existing composer and secondary actions remain intact.

The implementation also closes the final source-level regressions found during review: reminder overdue state refreshes on a bounded clock, clearing secondary filters preserves the selected queue, lease visibility is not hidden by SLA/reminder state, page state clamps/refetches when polling shrinks the result set, and zero-result changes are announced accessibly.
